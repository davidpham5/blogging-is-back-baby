It's hard to put into words the level of sadness and disgust I feel. Wednesday Jan 8th, 2026, Renee Nicole Good, mother of 3 and poet died in what can be described as a murder.

[Amanda Musa, Omar Jimenez, and Sarah Boxer of CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/01/08/us/renee-nicole-good-minneapolis-ice-shooting-hnk) had this to say regarding Good:

> A mother of three, Good had two children, ages 15 and 12, from her first marriage, The [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/ice-shooting-minneapolis-minnesota-9aa822670b705c89906f2c699f1d16c5) reported. Her 6-year-old child’s father died in 2023, according to the Star Tribune. “There’s nobody else in his life,” the child’s grandfather told the newspaper.
>
  Good also was a devoted Christian who took part in youth mission trips to Northern Ireland when she was younger, her ex-husband told the AP. She also loved to sing and participated in a chorus in high school before studying vocal performance in college, he said

He drew his weapon and executed her as she tried to leave. She was shot in the face. The vehicle slammed into a pole and a car. The airbag covered in blood, held Good's remains.
