At 80 years old, Dolly Parton has passed away. She was among one the most [admired living Americans](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/apr/14/dolly-parton-poll-most-popular-in-the-country?utm_campaign=heatmap_daily_paid&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-_YXoJ09GqYU_3JkcelrhIohD1tkD6m5B-w0Awi-M6SeAumkNNj3nOePTzPhlO4apsPzej0Q4GLZdkk2deibJpYNLhnVQ&_hsmi=435139269&utm_content=435139269&utm_source=hs_email), for several good reasons. Known for her music, with such hits as 9 to 5, her [Imagination Library](https://imaginationlibrary.com/) giving children books (over 332 million gifted), and [donating a million dollars for COVID vaccine research](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/03/02/world/dolly-parton-moderna-vaccine-covid.html?smid=nytcore-ios-share), she will be dearly missed and I for one mourn for her. 

One could say she lived the good life. She used her fame and wealth to help ordinary people in a thousand meaningful ways. She was a paid-up union member, American [Federation of Musicians, Local 257, Nashville](https://www.afm.org/2026/08/passing-of-dolly-parton/). [She gave a million of dollars](https://www.knoxnews.com/story/news/local/2024/10/04/dolly-parton-walmart-massive-donation-helene-flood-victims-east-tennessee/75511652007/) in disaster relief to good people in Sevierville, Tennessee after flooding left millions without power. 

Let her life be a reminder that there are millions of people like Dolly who fight for the everyday common folk of this country. With my little corner of the Internet, I anoint, first of her name, Dolly Parton, a Holy Patron Saint of the Web. Rest in Power, Dolly.
