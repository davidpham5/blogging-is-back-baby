date: 2025-09-26

We dream in the dark. Darkness everywhere we see. Blind to what is to come next. 

Fear and dread make our bed. Gentle heads slumber in the dead of night.

Do not wake me. I need to sleep deeply. I want to see what could be, dreams of democracy.
