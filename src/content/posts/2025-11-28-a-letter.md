*You and I belong to a special tradition. That tradition exercises a dormant power that exists in all of us. It awakens something that has slumber for far too long. Our workplace, as a system of hierarchies, works very hard to keep you out of important decisions. It's in constant motion and requires great energy to keep going. It's brittle to dissent, and cannot stand by itself without you. The Washington Post Tech Guild is eager to welcome you to be part of a powerful rite: going public as union. We have your back as you campaign for union recognition. You will win and celebrate. We hope you feel that profound joy and jubilation when you see each other *discover* your own power was there all this time.*
